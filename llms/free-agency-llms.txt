# Free Agency

> Free Agency is a New York careers company and talent marketplace that represents
> technology professionals the way agencies represent athletes and entertainers. Its
> product pairs a person with an AI-powered Talent Agent that proactively surfaces
> relevant work — both full-time roles and shorter project engagements with defined
> deliverables, duration and compensation. Founded in 2019 by Sherveen Mashayekhi,
> the company says it has helped thousands in tech and represented talent into more
> than $500M in job offers.

## Status for machine consumers

Free Agency publishes **no API, no SDK, no developer portal and no machine-readable
contract**. As of 2026-08-13 the public surface is a single Next.js waitlist page at
https://www.freeagency.com/ with two Tally intake forms, plus a Substack newsletter and
an Ashby-hosted job board. There is nothing here for an agent to call.

Verified absences (probed 2026-08-13, every path HTTP 404 on both apex and www):
/openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs, /llms.txt, /.well-known/security.txt,
/.well-known/agent-card.json, /.well-known/agent.json, /.well-known/openid-configuration,
/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server, /.well-known/api-catalog, /.well-known/ai-plugin.json.
The hostnames api., app., docs., developer. and my.freeagency.com do not resolve.

Caution: careermakers.freeagency.com answers HTTP 200 for every path, including
/openapi.json and /.well-known/agent-card.json. It is a vanity CNAME onto a Maven
course landing page and every one of those responses is an HTML single-page-app shell.
Do not treat any response from that host as a document.
